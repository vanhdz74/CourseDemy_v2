# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CourseDemy serves students browsing, buying, and learning online courses; teachers managing their course catalog and revenue; and admins operating users, categories, courses, payments, and reports. Users are usually task-focused: find a suitable course, continue learning, manage content, or resolve operational data quickly.

## Product Purpose

CourseDemy is an online learning platform that connects course discovery, checkout, course playback, and role-based management in one web app. Success means learners can confidently choose and continue courses, while teachers and admins can manage their work with clear, predictable interfaces.

## Brand Personality

Clean, capable, focused. The interface should feel like a modern EdTech product: bright, professional, easy to scan, and supportive without becoming decorative or noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid heavy gradients, excessive glass effects, over-rounded cards, nested card layouts, generic AI-looking SaaS decoration, and colorful visual noise. Do not replace API-driven data with static marketing content.

## Design Principles

Keep learning paths obvious: course cards, details, checkout, and lesson playback should make the next action clear.

Preserve operational clarity: dashboards, tables, and settings should prioritize scanning, comparison, and quick action.

Use restrained polish: improve spacing, typography, states, and hierarchy before adding decoration.

Respect existing behavior: visual upgrades must not change API contracts, auth flow, checkout flow, lesson routing, or role permissions.

Design responsively from the workflow outward: desktop can be dense, tablet should remain structured, and mobile should avoid cramped navigation or broken grids.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG-friendly contrast, visible focus states, readable type sizes, meaningful button/link labels, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and responsive layouts that remain usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
